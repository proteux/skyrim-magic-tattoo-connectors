# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- New Hair color effect: tints the actor's hair, and optionally their brows and beard, any color while a tier shows, with a gradual transition. Their own color comes back when the tier stops, and the console reset undoes it too. Works on KS Hairdos SMP wigs, and puts the color back if a wig is re-equipped or hair physics reset it.
- Log lines name the player instead of leaving the name blank.

## 0.1.0

- First release: the Actor scale effect, and the Body shape effect with 23 CBBE 3BA and TNG shapes, both with gradual transitions, and a console reset that undoes every change on the next load.
